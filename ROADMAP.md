# Roadmap

Everything we haven't built yet, ranked. Every item has a **priority**, an **area**, and a reason. Ideas wait here until they've earned a place on the screen (see [DOCTRINE.md](DOCTRINE.md) §6).

**How to use this file**

- **Priority.** **P0**: the app isn't really itself until this is done. **P1**: clear value; next in line. **P2**: good idea, no rush. Re-rank freely; that's the point.
- **Area.** One of: `TV` (getting onto and living on the television), `Voice`, `Remote` (phone, Siri, Alexa), `Pages`, `Ink`, `Content`, `Calendar`, `Weather`, `Photos`, `Infra`, `Privacy`.
- **Adding.** New ideas go in the right section with the next ID in their area. Say _why_ in one line. If it might break a law in the doctrine, say which and why it's worth it.
- **Finishing.** When something ships, delete its row and add a line to the log at the bottom. The code is the record of what exists; this file is only the record of what doesn't yet.

## Will not do

These come up. The answer is no, and here's why (doctrine §1).

- **News headlines, sports scores, stock tickers.** The world's news is an engagement machine; the app is about the family.
- **Notifications, badges, streaks, "you haven't checked…"** Nothing here reaches out to pull anyone in.
- **Analytics on attention** (time on screen, interactions). We don't measure looking.
- **Video autoplay, feeds, anything that scrolls.**
- **Ads, sponsored content, or the newsletter's promos.**
